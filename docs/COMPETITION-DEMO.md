# AIMS Competition Demo

Use clearly labelled synthetic data on an isolated local/demo database. Do not load real invoices, credentials, account details, or production records.

## Practical 10–12 minute flow

1. Initiate a request and show the system-generated ticket after capture/submission.
2. Upload a synthetic invoice and run AI-assisted extraction/validation, or explain the manual fallback.
3. Finalize Validation as a human and show the deterministic Finance Context values.
4. Show specialist risk recommendations and the human Final Risk Assessment.
5. Evaluate the deterministic configured Policy and show the resolved route.
6. Approve through web or Telegram; emphasize server-side identity and authority checks.
7. Complete mandatory Final Finance Control and reach `READY_FOR_PAYMENT`.
8. Record the already-executed external payment with an idempotency key and payment slip.
9. Show immutable Payment History and reconciled Dashboard totals.
10. Generate a Finance Watch interpretation and ask one bounded finance question with evidence links.
11. Switch AI Master OFF and show that the deterministic/manual workflow remains available.

No shortcut may pre-create a downstream stage or bypass Validation, Context, Risk, Policy, Approval, or Finance Control.

## Business value evidence

Without AIMS, requests may be fragmented across forms/messages, budget lookup and routing are manual, audit visibility is weak, and payment history/reporting are disconnected. AIMS demonstrates structured intake, deterministic budget context, configurable policy routing, audited human approvals, mandatory Finance Control, atomic payment recording, consolidated reporting, and optional evidence-backed AI interpretation.

Use measured system facts only: AI call/token/latency events, validation and approval timestamps, request-to-paid timestamps, clarification counts, deterministic context generation, and dashboard aggregation. If asked for time or cost savings, state `BASELINE NOT CONFIGURED` unless a measured comparison baseline has been supplied.
